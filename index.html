How the five money paths work

This page is the product surface. Live payouts need network accounts, traffic, and compliance.

Path 1

Niche affiliate links

You join Amazon Associates, ShareASale, CJ, Impact, etc. Replace each niche baseUrl in js/niches.js with your tracked link. You earn when visitors buy.

Path 2

Brand / company referrals

Many brands (Credit Karma, Booking, Coursera) run their own affiliate programs. Partner links under each card point at those companies with your ref codes.

Path 3

CPC / display ads

Networks pay per click or impression (e.g. AdSense). Paste their script into the dashed ad-slot regions. Approval often needs real content and traffic first.

Path 4

Sponsored placement

Sell the “Featured Partner” card as a flat-fee sponsorship, or put your best CPA offer there.

Path 5

Email / lead list

Capture emails, then monetize the list with promotions (or sell leads where legal). Connect the form to ConvertKit, Mailchimp, or a webhook when ready.
