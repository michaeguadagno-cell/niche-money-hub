Questions?

Will I get $9,000?

No one on this page can promise that. The $9,000 figure is a ballpark for two qualifying kids and about $17,500 of real earned income, using 2025-style IRS tables. You might get less, more, or nothing. The IRS tool is the check.

Is $17,500 a secret IRS cutoff?

No. It just sits near the strong band for the two-kid work credit. Fake “stay under $17,500 forever” posts are a myth.

Do I need an LLC?

An LLC does not raise the work credit by itself. Same tax as a simple side job for most people at this size.
